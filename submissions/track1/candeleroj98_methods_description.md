### 3. Please describe your model/approach in detail.

A rule-based, reproducible pipeline. It ingests SNVs and indels (Sentieon Haplotyper 202308.02 with GATK VariantFiltration) and Illumina Manta 1.6.0 structural variants. Sequence Ontology consequences are computed locally for the coding regions and ±2 bp splice junctions of all MANE Select transcripts. Alleles are filtered for rarity against gnomAD v4.1, paired into compound-heterozygous, homozygous or hemizygous candidates (phase undetermined), and ordered by information-content-weighted similarity to the child's HPO terms, with variant evidence (number of high-impact variants, then joint rarity) as tie-breaker. Structural variants are ranked only inside a curated 22-gene checkpoint panel. The ranking value is an ordinal score, not a probability. One primary prediction and nine secondary rows are submitted.

#### Abstract (272 words)
Mosaic variegated aneuploidy (MVA) is a rare, usually recessive disorder of the spindle assembly checkpoint. We present a rule-based, reproducible pipeline that prioritises recessive variants in one child sequenced with short reads. It ingests PASS SNVs and indels (Sentieon Haplotyper 202308.02 with GATK VariantFiltration) and Manta structural variants. Sequence Ontology consequences are computed locally for the coding regions and ±2 bp splice junctions of 19,293 MANE Select transcripts; they agreed with Ensembl VEP for 98.73 % of 23,035 variant-alleles. Alleles are filtered for rarity against gnomAD v4.1 (queried per allele; a failed query halts the run and is never read as absence), paired into compound-heterozygous, homozygous or hemizygous candidates, and ordered by information-content-weighted similarity to the child's HPO terms, with variant evidence as tie-breaker. Structural variants are ranked only inside a curated 22-gene checkpoint panel. The ranking value is an ordinal score, not a probability. A rare compound-heterozygous BUB1B pair (a stop-gain with predicted nonsense-mediated decay and a missense variant of uncertain significance) ranks first among five recessive candidates. The filters were adjusted after a first pass in which BUB1B ranked fifth of 22 candidate genes, so the sensitivity analyses describe the pipeline and do not validate it; without a rarity filter the pair ranks eighth. Phase is unresolved; dominant and de novo architectures, mosaic variants and megabase-scale structural calls were not analysed, and no sensitivity or specificity is claimed. All variant calling, annotation, scoring and the final file are produced by deterministic code; generative-AI tools were used to write and verify code, to audit reports and to edit the final report, and did not analyse the data or produce the ranking.
